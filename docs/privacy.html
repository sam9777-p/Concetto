Privacy Policy for Concetto'26

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 | Last Updated: September 30, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Concetto'26 ("we", "our", or "us"), the official annual techno-management festival of Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application (Package: com.iitismdhn.concetto).

By using the Concetto'26 app, you consent to the data collection and practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register, participate in events, or contact organizers:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, email address, phone number, college/institution name, and enrollment details provided during account creation and event sign-up.
  • Event Registrations & Team Data: Teams you create or join, member lists, events registered, and participation schedules.
  • Passes & Ticketing: Unique participant identifiers and digital QR pass data generated for fest entry.
  • Device Identifiers & FCM Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to deliver festival announcements, schedule changes, and security updates.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The app requests the following optional device permissions strictly for functional fest utilities:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely by fest organizers and attendees to scan QR codes on festival passes for verification. The app does not record, store, or stream photos or videos to external servers.
  • Notification Permission (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to deliver real-time festival notifications, broadcast alerts, schedule changes, and emergency updates. Users can disable notifications at any time via device settings.

4. Advertising ID Declaration

The Concetto'26 application does not display advertisements and does not collect, track, or share any Advertising ID (AAID).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We use reputable third-party services to securely host our backend infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging): Used to authenticate users, store event registration data, and deliver broadcast notifications. All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudinary: Used for secure media and image hosting. (Cloudinary Privacy Policy)

6. Data Security

We implement rigorous administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All communication between the app and cloud services occurs over secure HTTPS.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain user and registration data only for the duration necessary to manage Concetto festival operations and verify certifications. You have the right to request access to or deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us at the email provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for university students, school participants, and tech festival attendees aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
